### 29. `probe-particles.mjs`'s "does the flow draw" check could not pass, and the reason was its own control.

`web/probe-particles.mjs:150-190`. Not in the 2026-09-22 report because the probe was read, not
run; the handoff's verified list did not include it either.

The check takes two frames with the dot layer hidden, calls the difference between them a
**baseline** for background motion, and requires the layer's own difference to beat it
threefold. Measured on 2026-09-23 it failed twice in a row, at **3.71% against a 3.16%
baseline**.

Two separate faults, both found by measurement rather than by reading:

- **The baseline was the ray-marched water, not a control.** It waited 500 ms between the two
  hidden frames, and the volume's `uTime` moves **3.16%** of the frame in 500 ms while the whole
  dot layer is 2.7%. So the control was larger than the thing it controlled for and the rule
  could never pass. Proved by turning the volume off: the same pair then differs by **0.000%**.
- **Then the baseline was sometimes the layer itself.** With the water off and the waits
  removed, one run in two still came back at 2.67% - because 500 ms is not reliably longer than
  one swiftshader frame, so the screenshot after the hide sometimes still held the layer that
  had just been hidden.

Fixed by switching the water off for the pair, the way the markers already were, and by waiting
on two presented frames rather than on a number of milliseconds. Measured after, four runs:
the layer is **2.0 to 2.6%** against a **0.000%** baseline every time, and forcing the layer hidden for the signal frame
makes it go red, which is the project's own rule for a new check.

**This is the class `CLAUDE.md` already names** - *"two of the four needed the measurement
rethinking rather than an `exit` bolted on"* - and it is the reason the report's own finding 16
insisted on comparing two readings rather than printing them.

Verified after: **511 tests**, typecheck and build clean, `pyflakes` clean, `check_figures.py`
**0 current** over the widened checks.
